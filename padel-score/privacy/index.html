Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Padel Score is a Wear OS application developed by Mazuim Apps.

Data Collection

Padel Score does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Match and score information used by the app is stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to Mazuim Apps or third parties.

Data Storage and Deletion

App data is stored locally on the user's device for the purpose of providing the app's functionality.

Users can remove stored data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the application.

Third-Party Services

Padel Score does not currently use advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party services that collect personal information.

Children's Privacy

Padel Score does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data practices change. Any changes will be published on this page.
